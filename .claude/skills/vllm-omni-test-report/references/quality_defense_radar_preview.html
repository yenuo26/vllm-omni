Quality Defense Radar

Per-model 7-axis coverage radar across 10 flagship models (Functionality / Performance / Documentation / Precision / Stability / Reliability / Gate). Performance and Precision for Qwen3-Omni, MiniCPM, and HunyuanImage are the share of omni-reviewbot Model care rows covered by a case with the same dataset, full vs non-full duplex, feature, and hardware side, and that the CUDA or NPU nightly pipeline runs, or that is marked local_model. A random bench does not cover seed-tts, daily-omni, duplexEval, or OmniInteract. An npu mark does not count when NPU nightly never selects that test and it is not local_model. Other models fill a side when a test of that type is really run there, and leave it empty otherwise, with no percent caption. Reliability is the issue 133 library, including hang, overload, and dependency rows. Unimplemented cells stay in the denominator. Wan and Hunyuan multi-replica SIGKILL and hang cells are planned, not gaps. GPU halves are green, NPU halves are blue. Models without a Reliability axis render a 6-axis hexagon. Functionality, Documentation, Stability, and Gate are pre-lit from the current vllm-omni repo (54 binary segments on) and stay click-to-confirm. Clicking still stores a confirmation outline in localStorage and does not replace the computed percent.
